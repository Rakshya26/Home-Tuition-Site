Our Story
Built on love for learning.

Home Tuition Institute was founded with a simple but powerful belief: every child deserves personalised attention and the chance to flourish at their own pace. We bridge the gap between classroom learning and true understanding.

Our tutors are carefully vetted, passionate educators who don't just teach — they mentor, inspire, and adapt to each student's unique learning style.

From foundational subjects to advanced exam preparation, we are here for every step of your child's academic journey.

Our Journey
2010
Founded in Kathmandu with just 5 tutors and a vision.
2014
Expanded to Lalitpur and Bhaktapur districts.
2018
Launched online tutoring, reaching students nationwide.
2023
Celebrated 500+ students placed in top colleges.
