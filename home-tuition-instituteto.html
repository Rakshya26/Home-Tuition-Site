Our Story

Rooted in Purpose,
Growing Every Day

Home Tuition Institute was founded on a straightforward conviction: every student in the Kathmandu Valley deserves access to a skilled, reliable tutor — at home, on their schedule, and at a fair price.

We started as a small matching service connecting families with a handful of trusted educators. Over six years, we have grown into Kathmandu's most comprehensive tuition network, with verified tutors spanning every subject and grade level.

2018 · Foundation
A Small Beginning with a Big Vision
Launched with 8 tutors and 20 students in Lalitpur, offering maths and science home tuition.
2021 · Expansion
Scaling Across the Kathmandu Valley
Expanded into Bhaktapur and central Kathmandu, introduced online matching and SEE-specific programmes during the pandemic.
2024 · Today
